## Scope

I contributed to parts of MyJio and Jio AirFiber rather than owning either platform as a whole. My work included consumer mobile onboarding and interactions, Jio AirFiber purchase flows, and work involving ads and in-app purchases in MyJio. I also worked on internal role-based operations workflows.

## How I worked

I worked in Agile/Scrum with the product and engineering team, using Figma to explore flows, prototype interactions, and communicate design details. My experience also includes Adobe Illustrator for Jio advertising graphics, including work for brands such as Nestlé and Cadbury.
