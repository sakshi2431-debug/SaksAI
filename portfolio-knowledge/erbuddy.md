## My role and our scope

I was Design Lead, working with Claire Jen (Project Manager) and Karla Santamaria (Insights Lead). We focused on communication between patients and caregivers, including older adults and people with different levels of technical comfort. Research and final designs were team work; the Design Lead title does not mean I conducted every activity alone.

## What informed the design

We audited four existing flows: onboarding, editing a health profile, setting medication reminders, and creating an appointment. Our team conducted eight structured interviews with patients, caregivers, and supporters, then used affinity mapping and personas to make sense of their needs.

We narrowed the concept to switching between patient and caregiver roles and keeping a symptoms and feelings log. An AI note-taking idea was deferred after client feedback. It should not be described as a shipped AI feature.

## Testing and outcome

The team ran seven moderated sessions with medium-fidelity prototypes. Feedback exposed problems with terminology, medication logging, navigation, and account switching. We used that feedback to refine the high-fidelity screens and interface style guide.

The founder said ERbuddy intended to use our insights and screens in its Care Management Platform. That is client feedback and stated intent, not confirmation that the work shipped or produced a measured business result.
